    Home and our problem

    Business networking is sold at EBC Campus Chiapas as one of the best reasons to study here. But when we asked students, the same two doubts kept coming back.

    01

    Vague information

    Which companies? What kind of opportunities? How do I get in? How often?

    02

    Few local options

    The professional opportunities in the region feel limited compared to what is promised.
